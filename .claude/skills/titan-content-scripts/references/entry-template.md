## Notes on quality

- Every b-roll cell must be shootable with a phone, tripod, and mic by one person in the stated location.
- Keep spoken lines under ~20 words each; they are read aloud.
- On-screen text is 2–6 words plus at most one emoji.
- The Takeaway must pay off whatever the hook promised (reveal the answer, name the winner, show the result).

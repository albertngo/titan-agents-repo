# Hooks and angles

Adapted from blacktwist/social-media-skills (MIT): hook-writer-sms and content-repurposer-sms. Only the parts that fit Titan's shortform video workflow are kept.

## Hook patterns

Hook A is always **AEO**: a plain question someone would search for or ask an AI. Hooks B and C each use a *different* pattern from this list, and each one is labelled in the Option column, e.g. "Hook B (Contrarian)".

1. **Contrarian:** challenges common advice. "Stop steam-mopping your floors."
2. **Question:** specific and non-obvious. (Hook A covers the plain search question, so B and C need a sharper angle.)
3. **Story opener:** drops straight into a real moment. "This client almost ripped out perfectly good hardwood."
4. **Statistic / data:** a surprising, specific number. Titan tests leave it as XX until the shoot.
5. **List preview:** "3 things to bring when you pick a floor."
6. **Bold claim:** a declarative statement that the video then proves. "Laminate is the comeback kid of flooring."
7. **Empathy:** opens with the viewer's pain. "If your floors creak every time you walk to the kitchen, watch this."
8. **Before / after:** the transformation gap, ideally with a timeframe. Best for job-site content.
9. **Confession:** an honest admission that leads somewhere useful. "Honest truth: we talk people out of this floor all the time."
Plus native video framings: **Curiosity gap** ("wait for the reveal"), **POV:**, and **Pick one** challenges.

Hook rules for video:
- The hook is layered: spoken line, on-screen text, and the first visual all land in 1–3 seconds together.
- On-screen hook text stays under 6 words.
- The caption hook promises a different angle from the on-screen hook instead of repeating it.
- Hooks must be specific enough that they couldn't sit on anyone else's video.
- Pick a pattern only when the body can pay it off. A contrarian or bold claim hook needs proof in the Value section.

**Recommended pick:** under the hook table, add one line: `★ Recommended: Hook [X] — [one-sentence reason]`.

## Raw content mode: extracting angles

When the input is a job site, footage notes, a transcript, or a Project:

1. **Extract 3–10 standalone insights.** An insight qualifies only if it makes sense without the rest of the material. For each one, capture the core point in one sentence, the supporting beat (a shot, a quote, a number), and who it's for (new homeowner, condo owner, family with pets, and so on).
2. **Rank by standalone value.** Ask: "If someone only saw this one video, would it be worth 30 seconds?" If not, reframe it or merge it with another.
3. **Match each insight to a series:**
   - Transformation or reveal → Success Stories / Before-after
   - Process step → Behind the Scenes
   - Material close-up → Material Showcase
   - Mistake avoided or problem found → Spot the Problem
   - Client's own words → Client Q&A (real quotes only)
   - Design choice → Design Inspo / This or That?
4. The top-ranked insight becomes the anchor entry (Weight: Medium if it needs the full job-site footage); the rest become supporting entries.
5. One piece of footage can feed several entries. Note shared clips in each shot list ("reuse the stair reveal from [entry]") so Albert shoots them once.
